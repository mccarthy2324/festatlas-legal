# FestAtlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026/10/26

FestAtlas ("the app") is run by Nick McCarthy. Contact: festatlas1@gmail.com.

## What we collect

- **Account:** your email address and a password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase).
- **Profile (optional):** display name, handle, bio, location text, profile photo, and your privacy choices.
- **What you do in the app:** festivals and artists you track, sets you mark as seen or going, ratings, plans and checklists synced to your account, friend connections and requests, blocks and mutes, feedback you send, and reports you file.
- **Notifications:** if you allow push notifications, your device's notification token and your notification preferences.
- **Photos:** only the one photo you choose as your profile picture. We do not access your photo library otherwise.

## What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

## How it is used

To run your account and the app's features, show your activity to friends according to your privacy settings, send notifications you asked for, keep the service safe, and respond to support requests and reports.

## Who can see it

Your profile and activity are visible to other users according to your privacy settings (public or private, and what you share with friends). Blocked users cannot find you.

## Service providers

- **Supabase** hosts our database, authentication and file storage.
- **Apple** delivers push notifications.
- **Deezer and Open-Meteo:** to show artist photos and previews, the app requests them from Deezer; to show forecasts, it sends the festival's city to Open-Meteo. These requests are made from your device, so those services can see your IP address, as with any web request. We do not send them your account details.

## Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Profile, Delete Account). Deleting removes your profile, activity, tokens, photo and connections. Backups may retain copies for a short period. Reports you filed or that concern others may be kept where needed for safety.

## Children

FestAtlas is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Your choices

You can edit your profile, change privacy settings, turn off notifications (in the app or iOS Settings), and delete your account at any time. To ask for a copy of your data, email festatlas1@gmail.com.

## Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
